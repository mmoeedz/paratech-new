Create a premium, modern website for a full-service digital technology and growth agency that combines website design, web development, software development, digital marketing, SEO, and AI automation.

## Brand Positioning

Position the company as a premium **Digital Growth + Technology + AI Automation Agency**.

The agency helps businesses:

**Design → Build → Grow → Automate**

The website should communicate that the agency can create a business's digital presence, develop its technology, bring customers through digital marketing, and automate repetitive business operations with AI.

The company should feel like a **premium technology and creative studio**, not a generic web design agency and not a stereotypical futuristic AI startup.

## Visual Direction

Use a sophisticated **Obsidian + Warm Ivory + Copper** visual identity.

Primary colors:

- Obsidian Black: #0B0A09
- Deep Black: #151311
- Warm Ivory: #F3EFE7
- Copper: #B87333
- Dark Copper: #8F542D
- Muted Gray: #A8A29A
- Border: #2A2521

Use black/dark sections for high-impact areas and warm ivory sections for readability and contrast.

Copper should be used selectively for:
- Primary CTA buttons
- Small labels
- Section numbers
- Hover states
- Thin lines
- Workflow connections
- Important highlights
- Icons and small decorative elements

Do not overuse copper.

## Overall Design Style

Create a premium editorial technology aesthetic inspired by high-end digital agencies, modern technology companies, and sophisticated design studios.

The design should have:

- Large bold typography
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Minimal but sophisticated animations
- Thin borders
- Subtle gradients
- Fine grid/background details
- Smooth scrolling
- Elegant hover effects
- Micro-interactions
- Animated workflow diagrams
- High-quality case-study presentation
- Clean navigation
- Responsive design
- Excellent mobile experience

Avoid:
- Generic AI robot illustrations
- Brain graphics
- Excessive neon
- Purple/blue AI gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Cheap-looking 3D graphics
- Overly complicated animations
- Stock-photo-heavy layouts

The website should feel expensive, established, technical, and trustworthy.

## Typography

Use a premium modern sans-serif such as:

- Manrope

## Interactions

Use subtle premium animations:

- Text reveal on scroll
- Smooth section transitions
- Copper workflow lines that animate between nodes
- Cards that slightly move on hover
- Button hover animations
- Number counters where appropriate
- Subtle cursor interaction
- Animated AI workflow diagrams
- Parallax only where it improves the experience

Keep animations fast, smooth, and professional.

## Responsive Design

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop should feel spacious and editorial.

Tablet should maintain the visual hierarchy.

Mobile should simplify complex workflow diagrams into clean vertical flows.

Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.

## Final Brand Feeling

The visitor should immediately think:

**"These people can design our digital presence, build our technology, grow our business, and automate our operations."**

The final website should feel like:

**Premium Digital Agency + Technology Studio + AI Automation Partner**

It should look established, sophisticated, technically capable, and trustworthy while still being visually distinctive.

The final aesthetic should be approximately:

**60% premium digital studio  
25% technology company  
15% AI automation**

Do not make the website look like a generic AI SaaS landing page.
